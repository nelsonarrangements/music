PistonLink

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

PistonLink is an iPadOS and iOS app developed by Garrett Nelson ("we," "us," or "our") that helps organists manage stop registration profiles, browse curated hymn registrations, share and download registrations with other organists, and turn sheet music pages hands-free. This Privacy Policy describes what data PistonLink accesses, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

Data We Collect

PistonLink collects no personal data unless you choose to use the optional Catalog Your Organ feature or the optional Community, both described below. Everything else you create or import within the app — including registration profiles, sheet music PDFs, and organ configuration data — is stored on your device and leaves it only if you choose to share it, as described below.

We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, crash-reporting services, or any other technology that sends data off your device in the background. The app contacts our servers only when you use Catalog Your Organ, or open the Community or act in it.

Camera and Face Data

PistonLink requests access to your device's front-facing camera and TrueDepth sensor solely to power the hands-free page-turn feature. The app detects eye blinks in real time to advance or retreat sheet music pages. Camera and facial geometry data are processed entirely on-device using Apple's ARKit framework and are never stored, recorded, or transmitted anywhere.

MIDI and Bluetooth

PistonLink communicates with supported organs over MIDI (via USB or Bluetooth MIDI). MIDI messages sent and received are used only to read and write organ piston registrations in real time and are not logged or stored beyond the current session.

AI Hymn Registrant Feature

PistonLink includes an optional AI Hymn Registrant feature that uses Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device AI framework. When enabled, the feature processes hymn information you enter (hymn name or number and a character description) entirely on your device to generate organ stop registration suggestions. No data is sent to any server, and no information leaves your device. This feature requires a compatible device with Apple Intelligence enabled and is disabled by default.

Catalog Your Organ (Optional)

If your organ isn't supported yet, you can choose to catalog it: PistonLink walks you through your console and records the MIDI messages your organ sends as you draw and retire each stop. Your catalog stays on your device while you work on it. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send to PistonLink — it's entirely your choice, and you can close the catalog at any time without sending anything.

When you do choose to send a catalog, it includes:

  • Your organ's manufacturer, model, serial number, number of manuals and pistons, and the stop list you entered
  • The MIDI messages recorded from your organ during the catalog, and whether sending them back worked
  • Photos of the organ's nameplate and console (up to three console photos), if you added them
  • Your email address, which is required so we can follow up with questions about your organ
  • The PistonLink version you're using

This is used only to add support for your organ and to contact you with questions about it. It is delivered to us by email through Cloudflare and Resend, which process it only to deliver that email. We never sell it, share it with anyone else, or use it for marketing. We delete your email address and any photos from our own records within two weeks of your submission. Our email delivery provider, Resend, may keep delivery logs under its own retention practices. Technical organ data with no personal information attached — such as the manufacturer, model, stop list, and recorded MIDI messages — may be retained so we can support your organ and future users of the same model. To have your submission deleted sooner, reach us through the contact page.

If you take the nameplate or console photos with your camera, PistonLink uses the camera only for those photos; they are included only in a catalog you choose to send.

Community (Optional)

The Community lets you share registrations with other PistonLink users and download theirs. It needs no account, and you can use the rest of PistonLink without ever opening it.

Browsing and downloading. When you open the Community, PistonLink asks our server for the list of shared registrations. Downloading one saves a copy on your device so it works offline. As with any request over the internet, our server and our hosting provider, Cloudflare, can see your device's IP address while handling it. We do not store IP addresses with registrations, and Cloudflare handles them under its own privacy policy.

An anonymous install ID. The first time you download, upvote, report, or share, PistonLink creates a random identifier and keeps it on your device. It is not connected to your name, email address, Apple ID, or any other account. It is sent with those actions so that each download and upvote is counted once per install, to limit abuse, and to recognize repeat reports, and it is stored with the download, upvote, report, or submission it came with.

Sharing a registration. Nothing is shared unless you tap Send. When you do, we receive:

  • The hymn number and title
  • The registration's name and its verse names
  • The stops in each verse, including any Voice Palette choices
  • The organ model it was made for
  • The display name you typed, if you entered one

We never receive your notes, your organs or profiles, your email address, your photos, or anything else on your device. Each submission is emailed to us as a notice, through Cloudflare and Resend, so that we can review it. It stays hidden from everyone else until we approve it. Once approved, it is visible to all PistonLink users, with your display name if you gave one and anonymously if you did not. Please do not put personal information in a registration name or display name, since others can see it.

Upvotes and reports. Upvoting sends the registration's identifier and your install ID. Reporting also sends the reason you chose.

Keeping and removing. Approved registrations stay in the Community until we remove them. A submission we do not approve is never shown to anyone and may be deleted at any time. To have a registration you shared removed, reach us through the contact page with the hymn number and the registration's name. We can also remove a registration from other users' devices the next time their app connects. We never sell this data, share it with anyone else, or use it for marketing.

Files and Storage

PDF sheet music you import into PistonLink is stored in the app's local on-device container. Registration profiles and organ configurations you create are also saved locally, as are registrations you download from the Community. None of this data is backed up to any server operated by us, and only registrations you choose to share are ever sent to us; iCloud backups, if enabled by you in iOS Settings, are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

PistonLink does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app sends data only when a user chooses to use an optional feature: a catalog they submit (which includes an email address), a registration they share (which can include a display name they type), or the anonymous requests the Community makes to our server.
